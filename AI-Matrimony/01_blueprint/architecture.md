# Architecture Overview

हम एक advanced AI-driven matrimony platform बना रहे हैं जो Shaadi.com और BharatMatrimony के सभी अच्छे features के साथ-साथ गाँव-स्तर के matchmaker network और multi-level commission system भी संचालित करेगा।

**Main Highlights:**
- Website + WhatsApp-first onboarding
- Roles: End User, Matchmaker, Franchise (Taluka/District/State), Admin
- Commission splits: 70% to matchmaker, 2% referrer, 4% taluka, 3% district, 1% state, remainder company
- Wallet, monthly payouts, audit & KYC
- Plans: ₹500/1m, ₹1000/3m, ₹1500/6m, ₹2000/1y
- Hosting: WordPress (MVP), Hostinger
